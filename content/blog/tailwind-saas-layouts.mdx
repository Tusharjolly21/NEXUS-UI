## Density without noise

Premium SaaS interfaces balance information density with calm whitespace. Use consistent radii (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`) and translucent surfaces instead of flat gray fills.

### Layered borders

Combine `border-white/10` with `bg-white/5` and `backdrop-blur-xl` for glass panels. Add an inner highlight with `shadow-[inset_0_1px_0_rgba(255,255,255,0.06)]`.

### Typography rhythm

Pair a tight display size on heroes with relaxed body copy. Keep line length between 60–78 characters for long-form marketing.

Use OKLCH neutrals for smoother gradients and cleaner dark mode transitions.
