## The section stack

1. **Hero** — promise, proof, primary CTA, secondary exploration CTA.
2. **Social proof** — logos, metrics, or compliance callouts.
3. **Product depth** — bento or split layouts showing workflows.
4. **Templates or components** — show the actual UI, not abstract icons.
5. **Pricing** — transparent tiers with a highlighted recommendation.
6. **FAQ + final CTA** — remove last objections and re-state the bet.

Ship each section as an isolated block so you can reorder or A/B test without rewiring the entire page.
